Privacy Policy

MigraineAI — Migraine Tracker with AI Insights
Last updated: June 2026

1. Overview

MigraineAI ("the App") is a migraine tracking application developed by Samuel Hudac ("we", "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data. The App does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name.

2. Data We Collect

The App stores the following types of data, all of which you enter yourself:

  • Migraine records: Date, time, pain intensity, duration, triggers, symptoms, medications taken, pain locations (head map), relief rating, time to relief, and free-text notes.
  • Daily check-ins: Optional daily entries for hours of sleep, water intake, stress level, and mood, used to identify protective factors.
  • Menstrual cycle information (optional): If you enable cycle tracking, the App stores your last period start date, cycle length, and period length to correlate migraines with your cycle. This feature is entirely optional and disabled by default.
  • Health profile: Your usual medications and known triggers, used to speed up logging.
  • Self-assessments: Your answers and scores for the MIDAS and HIT-6 migraine disability questionnaires.
  • Approximate location: Used solely to fetch local weather data (barometric pressure, humidity, temperature) at the time of logging a migraine. Location is never stored, shared, or transmitted to our servers.
  • Weather data: Barometric pressure, humidity, and temperature are stored locally on your device as part of your migraine record.
  • App preferences: Language and theme settings, notification preferences, onboarding completion status, achievement progress, and subscription status.

3. Data Storage

All your health data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate any account system, server, or cloud database, and we do not upload or sync your records anywhere. Data entered into the App is retained locally on your device until you delete it using the "Clear all data" option, uninstall the App, or clear the App's storage through your device settings. We do not retain any copy of your health records on our servers.

4. Backup & Export

The App lets you create a backup file of your data and restore it later, and to generate PDF reports. These files are created only when you choose to, and are saved or shared using your device's own sharing tools — for example to your files, email, or cloud storage of your choice. We never receive a copy. Once a backup, export, or report leaves the App, it is under your control, and you are responsible for storing it securely.

5. AI Analysis

The App offers AI-powered insights as an optional Pro feature. When you use this feature:

  • Basic insights are generated entirely on your device using local algorithms. No data is sent anywhere.
  • Deep AI insights (Pro feature) send a summary of recent migraine records — specifically dates and times, intensity, duration, triggers, symptoms, and medications — to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. We do not include any directly identifying information such as your name, email, exact location, or your free-text notes in these requests.

You can use the App without the AI feature, and no migraine data will be transmitted externally.

6. Notifications

The App can send local reminders and alerts directly from your device — for example daily check-in reminders, medication reminders, weather-based risk alerts, medication-overuse warnings, and monthly summaries. These notifications are generated on your device; no notification content is sent to us or any third party. You can disable notifications at any time in the App or in your device settings.

7. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Displays advertisements in the free version. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data according to Google's Privacy Policy. Pro subscribers do not see ads.
  • RevenueCat: Manages in-app subscriptions and entitlements. RevenueCat processes purchase data according to their Privacy Policy.
  • Open-Meteo: Provides weather data based on approximate coordinates. Open-Meteo does not receive any personal information. See their Terms.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): Processes anonymized migraine summaries for AI insights (Pro feature only). See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • EmailJS: Delivers feedback messages if you choose to send feedback through the App. Only the message text and your optional email address are transmitted.

8. Advertising

The free version of the App displays banner and interstitial advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may use advertising identifiers and device information to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings. Pro subscribers enjoy an ad-free experience.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal or health data with any third party. The only data transmitted externally is:

  • Approximate coordinates to Open-Meteo (for weather, not stored by them)
  • Anonymized migraine summaries to Anthropic (for AI insights, Pro only)
  • Standard advertising data to Google AdMob (free version only)
  • Purchase and subscription data to RevenueCat and Google Play (when you subscribe)
  • Feedback messages to EmailJS (only when you explicitly send feedback)

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete all data: Use the "Clear all data" option in the App's Settings to permanently delete all your records from your device.
  • Export your data: Use the backup or PDF export feature to generate a copy of your records.
  • Opt out of ads: Subscribe to Pro or adjust your device's advertising settings.
  • Revoke location access: Disable location permission in your device settings at any time. The App will continue to work without weather tracking.
  • Disable optional features: Cycle tracking, notifications, and AI insights are all optional and can be turned off at any time.

12. Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's built-in security (screen lock, encryption). We do not have access to your data. Any data transmitted to a third-party service (weather, AI analysis, feedback, or purchases) is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
