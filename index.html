Privacy Policy

MigraineAI — Migraine Tracker with AI Insights
Last updated: May 2026

1. Overview

MigraineAI ("the App") is a migraine tracking application developed by Samuel Hudac ("we", "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

2. Data We Collect

The App collects the following types of data:

  • Migraine records: Date, time, pain intensity, triggers, symptoms, medications, notes, and duration. This data is entered manually by you.
  • Approximate location: Used solely to fetch local weather data (barometric pressure, humidity, temperature) at the time of logging a migraine. Location is never stored, shared, or transmitted to our servers.
  • Weather data: Barometric pressure, humidity, and temperature are stored locally on your device as part of your migraine record.
  • App preferences: Language settings, onboarding completion status, and subscription status.

3. Data Storage

All your health data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload, sync, or store your migraine records on any external server or cloud service. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, all records are permanently deleted.

4. AI Analysis

The App offers AI-powered insights as an optional Pro feature. When you use this feature:

  • Basic insights are generated entirely on your device using local algorithms. No data is sent anywhere.
  • Deep AI insights (Pro feature) send anonymized migraine data (dates, intensity levels, triggers, symptoms) to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. No personally identifiable information (name, email, exact location) is included in these requests.

You can use the App without the AI feature, and no data will be transmitted externally.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Displays advertisements in the free version. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data according to Google's Privacy Policy. Pro subscribers do not see ads.
  • RevenueCat: Manages in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat processes purchase data according to their Privacy Policy.
  • Open-Meteo: Provides weather data based on approximate coordinates. Open-Meteo does not receive any personal information. See their Terms.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): Processes anonymized migraine data for AI insights (Pro feature only). See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • EmailJS: Processes feedback messages if you choose to send feedback through the App. Only the message text and your optional email are transmitted.

6. Advertising

The free version of the App displays banner and interstitial advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may use advertising identifiers and device information to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings. Pro subscribers enjoy an ad-free experience.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal or health data with any third party. The only data transmitted externally is:

  • Approximate coordinates to Open-Meteo (for weather, not stored by them)
  • Anonymized migraine patterns to Anthropic (for AI insights, Pro only)
  • Standard advertising data to Google AdMob (free version only)
  • Feedback messages to EmailJS (only when you explicitly send feedback)

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete all data: Use the "Clear all data" option in the App's Settings to permanently delete all your migraine records.
  • Export your data: Use the PDF export feature to generate a report of your records.
  • Opt out of ads: Subscribe to Pro or adjust your device's advertising settings.
  • Revoke location access: Disable location permission in your device settings at any time. The App will continue to work without weather tracking.

10. Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's built-in security (screen lock, encryption). We do not have access to your data. For AI analysis requests, data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
